### Checked and found correct (no finding)

- **Limit inclusivity matches the docs.** `amount == perTxMax` is approved, `dailyTotal + amount == dailyMax` is approved, and `amount == requireApprovalAbove` is auto-approved (strictly above needs approval). 50.01 against a 50 threshold gives `needs_approval`; 100.0000001 against perTxMax 100 is rejected.
- **Bad amounts are rejected** with `invalid_amount`: 0, -0, negative, NaN, ±Infinity, the string `"10"` and `null`. Payee matching is exact and case-sensitive (fail-closed).
- **State transitions through orchestrator functions are correct:** approve or reject after paid gives `already_paid`; approve after reject, a second reject, and approve on an auto-approved entry give `not_pending` / `already_paid`; an unknown id gives `intent_not_found`. Concurrent double approve pays only once.
- **reconcile and refresh** handle a throwing or mismatched `status()` without writing, report the error, keep the entry pending and holding the limit, and are idempotent.
- **resolve** goes through `settleReceipt` with CAS on `pending`. `failed` frees budget and `succeeded`/`refunded` keep counting, as documented.
- **Atomic replace works:** no torn `ledger.json` in 15 SIGKILL trials.

## Proposed fix order

1. **F1 + F11:** atomic evaluate-and-record. This is internal-first, and it closes the confirmed overspend.
2. **F7:** fail-closed policy and total validation (small, additive).
3. **F12:** map CAS losses to `PaymentError` (trivial, low risk).
4. **F8 + F13 + F10:** file lock, fsync, tmp cleanup, schema validation on read, `.bak` file (internal durability).
5. **F4 part 1 + F5:** single payment-and-receipt write; optional timeouts.
6. **Lead-approval batch:** F2 (server-time day bucket), F3 (unknown outcome holds the limit), F4 parts 2 and 3 (in-flight state and operator resolve for stuck entries), F9, F6, F15, F16.
7. **F14:** idempotency normalization, and a stable id in the agent client.
